Your arduino must have a DAC.

SD Card Module to Arduino: 

Gnd to Gnd
VCC to Vin
MISO to 12
MCSI to 11
SCK to 13
CS to 10

Arduino to earbuds plug (the earbuds have three sections):

The tip is for the left earbud while the middle is for the right earbud, these two can touch each other, if the wire slips
off one of these, then music won't be heard through the corresponding earbud. 
These two connect to a small ceramic capacitor (like 104) and a resistor maybe? and then to the A0 pin of the arduino.

The bottom of the plug is gnd so it connects directly to the arduino gnd.
You can just strip the wires and wrap them around the plug.
