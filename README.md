It's gonna be an mp3 pla0oyeerwe. 

I'm gonna use old earbuds, a arduino, lcd screen, microsd card and module, joystick and button for controls, 4 double a battery pack, and cardboard case. 

I learned that the tip of earbuds actually wire to their own things. 
the very tip is for the left earbud, the middle is for the right earbud, and 
the very bottom is gnd. 

the arduino i have is special and can play audio to the earbuds without a driver i just need a resistor and a capacitor. 

The LCD screen will be the menu and the joystick will be used to go through the menu. Clicking the joystick itself will be A and clicking
the button will be B. 

im gonna put the songs on the microsd, and categorize them so you can select through them. maybe ill add playlist maybe its too hard 
and ill not fdo it.
